# Market Rule Lab 🧪

> *The experimental ground for data-driven financial research and hypothesis validation.*

## Overview
**Market Rule Lab** serves as the research and development environment for the broader systematic trading ecosystem. It is designed for exploratory data analysis (EDA), pattern discovery, and rigorous empirical modeling.

## Core Objectives
- **Hypothesis Generation:** Identify market anomalies, structural inefficiencies, and potential trading signals.
- **Model Validation:** Stress-test mathematical models against vast datasets to rigorously assess statistical significance, robustness, and risk profiles.
- **Data Analytics:** Leverage statistical inference and machine learning pipelines to parse market noise and extract mathematical edge.

## Relationship with the Ecosystem
Primary sandbox for logic simulation and structural validation. Once a workflow passes initial verification, its configuration schemas are synced to `market-rule-grimoire` for centralized state management, facilitating downstream integration and runtime execution via `market-rule-forge`.
